Entry for the make.fun bounty **One Letter for Your Day**: send exactly one
letter, A–Z, standing for the word that describes your day.

This entry sends the letter **O — for Overdrive**: a day of nonstop bounty
building that ran straight through midnight and kept going, an engine pushed
past its limit and never dropping back down a gear.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, stdlib only, deterministic, no network) writes that
single letter to `/output/entry.txt` and verifies it is exactly one byte,
one uppercase letter A–Z, with no trailing newline.
